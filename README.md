# AI Governance Center

An independently designed platform for translating applicable EU AI Act requirements into accountable work and reviewable evidence.

Status: design baseline 0.1, proposed on 13 September 2026. No executable application or complete legal catalogue is included yet. This baseline records proposals, not user approval of every design choice.

## Start here

- [Product and legal coverage approach](docs/01-product-and-coverage.md)
- [Information model and workflows](docs/02-information-and-workflows.md)
- [Architecture and delivery plan](docs/03-architecture-and-delivery.md)

The first implementation milestone is a complete inventory-to-evidence workflow. Full EU AI Act coverage remains the target; it must be demonstrated through a provision-level coverage register and validated rules.

## Project principles

- This project is independent of the earlier “AI governance tool” project. Evaluate ideas on their merits; do not inherit its implementation or constraints.
- Separate legal sources, interpretations, product controls, and case-specific decisions.
- Preserve uncertainty and missing information. Do not convert unanswered questions into negative answers.
- Preserve the versions of facts, rules, and evidence behind each approved decision.
- Commit documentation and tested changes at meaningful milestones. Keep operational evidence and credentials outside Git.

## Current open decisions

Initial deployment assumes one customer installation with multiple legal entities. Multi-customer SaaS, implementation framework, and hosting provider remain undecided. These assumptions are detailed in the architecture document.
